# Changelog — DiDo terminal

Tags: `dido/vX.Y.Z`. Versioned independently of the other components; see `versions.json` for the current set.
Each release lists what changed for users and integrators.

## [0.31.0] - 2026-10-13

- The start page moves more calmly: the trending row scrolls slower and the
  token field drifts more gently.
- Two small glass DigitalDon figures now sit in the start page's margins on
  wide screens.

## [0.30.0] - 2026-10-13

- The top-left corner now reads **DiDo**, the platform's name, next to the
  logo.
- On the start page, a small glass globe in the bottom-left corner opens
  into DigitalDon's website, X, Telegram, GitHub, docs and token listing
  when you point at it (tap on phones).

## [0.29.0] - 2026-10-12

- The chart can switch between price and market cap.
- The trending row shows just each token's ticker and its move; hover a
  token for the details.

## [0.28.0] - 2026-10-12

- The start page's filters can show established projects and small ones
  apart; both show together by default.
- The trending row above the field is slimmer and quieter, so the field
  stands out.

## [0.27.0] - 2026-10-12

- The watchlist shows each token's logo next to its name.
- The start page's field loads under the same DigitalDon mark as the holder
  map, filling as it reads.

## [0.26.1] - 2026-10-12

- The start page is centred and symmetric on every screen.
- The trending row reliably starts moving again once you move the mouse
  away from it.

## [0.26.0] - 2026-10-12

- DiDo's start page is one clean screen: the search in the top bar, a
  trending row over the field, your watchlist and your recent tokens in two
  rows under it.
- The trending row moves on its own like a ticker; point at it to stop it
  and scroll with the mouse wheel to look through it. Every row scrolls with
  the wheel.

## [0.25.2] - 2026-10-12

- The holder map behind the terminal is calmer: zooming into a group,
  dragging or clicking now settles quickly and then stays perfectly still.
- Zoom glides smoothly toward the cursor instead of jumping in steps.

## [0.25.1] - 2026-10-12

- Scroll with the mouse wheel over your recent tokens to run through them.
- The trail of tokens you visited now shows only while you look at a token.

## [0.25.0] - 2026-10-12

- DiDo's start page is now one calm stage: the field of every token we read,
  or the picks as cards, switched from one floating control. Nothing on the
  page moves while it loads, and the whole home fits your screen, phone or
  desktop.
- Scroll with the mouse to zoom into the field.
- Your watchlist and recent tokens sit under the stage with their logos,
  live prices and what to do now.
- The greeting now says the state of the day in a plain sentence.

## [0.24.1] - 2026-10-12

- The start page's field now shows every token we read by default, with the
  picks highlighted among them. Hover any bubble to see why it was or was
  not picked. "The board" still shows the picks alone.

## [0.24.0] - 2026-10-12

- The start page has a chain filter: show only Solana, only EVM chains, or
  pick chains one by one. The picks, the busiest tokens, the groups' calls
  and the field follow it, and your choice is remembered on this device.
  Your watchlist and recent tokens always show in full.

## [0.23.0] - 2026-10-11

- The start page and its field show a new kind of pick, "at support": tokens
  resting on a support zone with a wide room to their sell level, marked with
  a dashed ring and their score.
- Highlighted tokens are now always drawn larger than every other bubble in
  the field.
- When the share held by a token's ten largest holders is an estimate, the
  start page says "at most".

## [0.22.2] - 2026-10-11

- The token bubble that travels from a wallet to the loading screen now
  has clean, sharp edges.

## [0.22.1] - 2026-10-11

- The holder map shows every wallet of a connected group, including those
  ranked below the top holders, marked with a dotted ring.

## [0.22.0] - 2026-10-11

- DiDo's trail stays a compact pill on long journeys: it shows where you
  are and the step before, and opens to the whole way on hover, with
  arrows to slide through a long one. It no longer runs under the chart.

## [0.21.1] - 2026-10-11

- Opening a token from a wallet now shows a tidy loading screen: the
  token's logo with the progress and phase beneath it, instead of the logo
  sitting over the loader.
- When holder data cannot be loaded, DiDo now says so instead of staying
  on the loading screen.

## [0.21.0] - 2026-10-11

- On DiDo's home garden, clicking a token bubble opens a small menu with
  "scan token" and "look closer", so a token can be scanned straight from
  the field without zooming in first.

## [0.20.0] - 2026-10-11

- A wallet in DiDo now shows its bubbles right in the wallet panel, with the
  map still beside it.
- "Full view" on the bubbles' corner opens them over the whole screen, and
  the same chip takes you back to the map.

## [0.19.0] - 2026-10-11

- New "running hot" shelf on the start page: solid tokens trading busier
  than their usual hour right now, refreshed live. It shows trading activity,
  not a buy call.
- The start page explains three new reasons a token was left out: almost no
  sells, far more traded than its pool can take, or too few trades.

## [0.18.0] - 2026-10-11

- Opening a wallet in DiDo now shows it as bubbles in the holder map's glass:
  one bubble per holding, sized by its share, grouped by chain, with the
  list beside it. The bar on the wallet's edge shows the chain split and
  folds the bubbles away.
- Click any holding, bubble or row, for a menu that scans that token,
  highlights it, filters to its chain or copies its contract.
- Whatever you pick glides into the middle and becomes the next view, and a
  trail at the bottom of the screen takes you back to any token or wallet
  you visited.

## [0.17.1] - 2026-10-11

- The start page says which safety check a left-out token could not finish.
- The security facts at the top of a token now appear (they had no data
  before).

## [0.17.0] - 2026-10-09

- On DiDo's start page you can now zoom into the field of tokens: click to
  look closer, drag to move, pinch on a phone. Closer in, each token shows
  its name, its entry range and where the price sits; closest, it shows the
  shape of its holders.
- Click a token once to look at it, twice to open it.
- The back button now returns from a token to the start page.

## [0.16.0] - 2026-10-09

- DiDo's start page now opens on a living field of every token the engine
  checked: tokens at their entry glow, the rest show why they were left out.
  Point at one to see it, click to open it.
- The familiar cards sit one scroll below the field, and you can choose
  whether DiDo opens on the field or on the cards.

## [0.15.1] - 2026-10-09

- When no token is at its entry, the start page now says so plainly and shows
  how many tokens were checked and when the next check runs.

## [0.15.0] - 2026-10-08

- DiDo's start page now opens with the tokens sitting at their entry right
  now, picked by the DigitalDon engine every few hours, with each card
  showing the entry zone, the price and the sell level on one line.
- A second row shows tokens just above their entry, so you can wait for a
  dip. Nothing on these rows is paid for.

## [0.14.1] - 2026-10-08

- The DigitalDon logo at the top left of the terminal now takes you back to
  the terminal's start page with the trending tokens, instead of opening the
  website.

## [0.14.0] - 2026-10-05

- Tokens that trade only in less common pairings, for example against a
  tokenized stock or another coin instead of SOL or a stablecoin, now get a
  full score, chart and trade plan. They used to stop at "not enough chart
  history" even after days of active trading.

## [0.13.0] - 2026-10-04

- DiDo opens on a welcome: your watchlist with what to do on each token,
  the tokens you opened last, and the tokens the DigitalDon bot's groups
  called most in the last 24 hours, with the move since the first call.
  One click opens any of them.
- Over a token, opening the empty search lists your recent tokens.
- Fixed: the copy button beside a token's name now copies its contract
  address.

## [0.12.1] - 2026-10-04

- The links window shows only the project's own links, exactly as the
  project lists them: its order, its button names, no empty slots.
- Keyboard focus stays on the map when it finishes loading.

## [0.12.0] - 2026-10-04

- The wallet pane shows holdings and totals sooner; profit and loss fill in a
  moment later.

## [0.11.0] - 2026-10-04

- A links button next to the token's name opens the project's own links

  each one goes before you click. Press `L` to open it.
- The window also says which basic links a project does not list.

## [0.10.2] - 2026-10-04

- The top bar's token pill now reads: logo, token name, ticker, chain, copy

## [0.10.1] - 2026-10-04

- On the start screen the search sits large in the middle again; after

## [0.10.0] - 2026-10-04

## [0.9.0] - 2026-10-04

## [0.7.0] - 2026-10-03

## [0.4.2] - 2026-10-02

## [0.4.1] - 2026-10-02

## [0.4.0] - 2026-10-02

- Fixed the holder map sometimes reading "not live" right after the terminal
  was updated, even though it was available.
- The chart and the rest of the terminal are usable while the holder map is
  still loading, instead of waiting behind it.
- The holder map makes room for the chart, the watchlist and an open wallet:
  bubbles arrange themselves around the panels instead of hiding behind them.
- Smoother all round: dragging panels, hovering and panning the chart, and

- **Arrange the terminal your way:** drag the chart, the watchlist or a
  wallet by its title bar to anywhere on screen. Your layout is remembered;
  double-click a title bar (or press 0) to put things back.

## [0.3.0] - 2026-10-02

- **New look:** the terminal now has the same glass design as the analyzer.
- **Watchlist in the terminal:** star any token and see, at a glance, which
  ones are in their buy zone, waiting for a dip, under the zone or showing no
  buy signal. Lists shared from the analyzer open here too.
- **Open any holder's wallet:** on the holder map, an EVM wallet opens beside
  the map with its whole portfolio across chains, and any token it holds
  opens as its own terminal. The browser's back button retraces the path.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-09-18

  of the field free for the upcoming trade bar. Wallet funding times remain
  visible as the rug along the bottom of the chart, against price and time.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-18

- DiDo, the DigitalDon terminal, is a new product: the wallet cluster map
  fills the whole page and everything else sits on top of it, with the same
  dossier, replay and keyboard shortcuts as the analyzer.
- A chart docked over the map shows all four plan bands (short-term entry
  and exit, long-term entry and target) switchable from the legend, plus
  volume, optional 20/50 EMAs and funding times of mapped wallets. It zooms,
  pans, measures moves between two points, resizes or folds away, and
  becomes a sheet on phones.
- A security strip shows honeypot, mint, freeze, owner and tax checks;
  missing data reads as missing, never as safe. Live price refreshes every
  30 s with a status dot, and a $DON house-policy badge appears when it
  applies.
- Every view is a link: the token, a search, the timeframe and chart size
  all live in the URL. Arc is supported from launch.
